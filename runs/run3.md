## Snapshot

Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) is an Indian manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. For Q1 FY27 (April-June 2026), the company reported a revenue of ₹1,248 crore and a net profit of ₹82 crore [S3]. The company is expanding its capacity with a new plant in Bharuch, expected to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3].

## Bull case

Sarvottam Cables is experiencing strong demand from power transmission and data-center projects [S3]. The company's order book increased to ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [S3]. Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, an increase from 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3]. The company's new Bharuch plant is expected to add approximately 30% to its cable capacity and is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [S3]. Management expects this plant to contribute from the second half of FY27 and maintains a revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17% for the full year FY27 [S2]. Data-center cables, currently 9% of domestic revenue, are projected to double in two years [S2]. While operating margins were affected by rising copper prices, management expects to pass on these costs to customers over the next two quarters, as about two-thirds of their contracts have price-variation clauses that reset with a lag [S2, S3]. The company's net debt to equity is 0.35 times, with the Bharuch capex mainly funded through internal accruals and some term debt [S2]. Promoter pledging has decreased to 4.1% of promoter holding as of June 2026, down from 11.5% in June 2025 [S8].

## Bear case

Despite a rise in revenue, Sarvottam Cables' operating margins contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 due to a sharp increase in copper prices [S1, S3]. While management expects to pass on these costs, there is a lag of one to two quarters for price-variation clauses to reset [S2]. Receivable days have increased from 78 to 96 over the last year, primarily due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [S2]. The company recently received a GST demand order for ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) related to input tax credit for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. Although the company intends to appeal and does not expect a material impact, this represents a potential financial liability [S7].

## Open questions

- There is a discrepancy in reported revenue for Q1 FY27. Business Daily Online reported revenue from operations as ₹1,428 crore [S1], while the company's official press release stated ₹1,248 crore [S3]. Both sources refer to the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The company's official press release [S3] is preferred as the primary source for financial figures.
- The news article from Market Watch India [S4] from March 2024 stated that promoter pledging had risen to 35% of their holding. However, the NSE shareholding pattern filing [S8] as of June 2026 shows promoter pledging at 4.1% of promoter holding. This suggests a significant reduction in pledged shares, but the reason for this change and the discrepancy with the earlier news report is not fully explained in the provided documents.
- The local news article about "Sarvottam Cable Network" being penalized in Nagpur [S6] appears to refer to a different entity than Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE), the manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors [S3]. The article mentions a local cable TV and broadband operator and its owner, Mr. Suresh Patil, which does not align with the profile of SRVCABLE. This source is therefore excluded from the analysis of SRVCABLE.
